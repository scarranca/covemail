About you, on all your devices

  • About you can now sync between Cove on your Mac, iPhone and iPad. Turn on “Sync with your iPhone and iPad” in Agent → About you; it’s off until you do, and the newest edit wins.
  • It doesn’t need cloud mail sync and sends no email. About you is stored encrypted on Cove’s server, which can decrypt it, and you can remove the server copy at any time.
