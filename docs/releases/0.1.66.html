Attach files

  • Attach files to new emails and replies with the paperclip beside Send, or drop them anywhere on a draft.
  • While you’re writing a reply, drop files anywhere on the window to attach them; with nothing open, dropping files starts a new email with them.
  • Files are kept with the draft on this Mac, encrypted, until the email is sent, so Undo and restarts don’t lose them. Gmail allows 25 MB per email.
