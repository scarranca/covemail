Typing that keeps up

  • Typing a reply or a new email is much faster: each letter no longer redraws the whole email and conversation around it.
  • The To and Subject fields respond at once, even in a large mailbox.
  • Contact suggestions in To appear instantly, and opening a new email no longer pauses to gather your contacts.
  • iPhone: contact suggestions in To are faster and no longer stall when new mail arrives.
