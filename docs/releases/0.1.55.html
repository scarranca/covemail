Keep working while Cove syncs

  • Archive, star, label and connect Calendar or Tasks while Cove catches up: syncing now runs quietly in the background.
  • Your changes show instantly and reach Gmail in order; a sync never undoes them, and if Gmail refuses one, only that change is undone.
  • Writing is much faster: drafts save after a short pause instead of on every keystroke.
  • Accents and other composed characters (á, ñ, ü) no longer send the cursor to the start.
  • Send now waits 4 seconds with an Undo bar, like Delete, instead of asking first.

Nothing is sent, archived or deleted without you.
