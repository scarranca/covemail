Faster writing, and everyone in To

  • Typing a new email or a reply, moving between emails and opening a conversation are faster: Cove no longer re-scans the whole mailbox as you go.
  • The To field suggests anyone you’ve emailed, not only recent mail. After a short pause, Cove looks up matching names and addresses in Gmail; it never reads the emails’ text for this.
  • Loading older mail, label views, search results and images inside emails is faster.
