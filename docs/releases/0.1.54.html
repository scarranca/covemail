Smoother catch-up

  • Cove now stays within Gmail's limits: after time away it catches up in small, steady batches, newest mail first, instead of hitting a wall.
  • Label changes from other devices sync without downloading those emails again, so catching up is much lighter.
  • If Gmail asks Cove to slow down, it keeps what it already has and continues on its own a minute later, with a quiet status instead of an alert.
  • Background downloads go gently on battery and faster when your Mac is plugged in.
  • Connecting Calendar or Tasks during a sync now works: Cove connects as soon as the sync finishes.

Nothing is sent, archived or deleted without you.
