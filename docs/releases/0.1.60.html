More than one account

  • Sign in to several Google accounts and switch between them from the account menu (or ⌃1–⌃9). Each keeps its own mail, agents, tasks and From address.
  • Add a work account with your organization's own Google client, so your whole Google Workspace can sign in.
  • While Google sign-in waits, a bar lets you copy the link for another browser, open it again, or cancel.
  • Agent activity on the Agents screen now leads with the label, so long subjects no longer hide it.

Your current sign-in moves over automatically the first time you open this version.
