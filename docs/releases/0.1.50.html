Faster search, faster drafts

  • Search is instant: matches appear as you type, even across thousands of downloaded emails.
  • Drafting is quicker. Simple rewrites skip planning, and with a ChatGPT subscription drafts stream in as they’re written.
  • Follow-ups like “make it warmer” reuse the emails Cove already found instead of searching Gmail again.
  • Each email is now stored as its own encrypted record on your Mac, so syncing saves only what changed. Your existing mailbox is converted automatically the first time you open this version.

After updating, earlier versions of Cove can’t open the converted mailbox. Nothing is sent, archived or deleted without you.
