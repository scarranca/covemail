Your next meeting, in the menu bar

  • Turn on Meetings in the menu bar (Settings → Gmail) to see your next meeting at a glance: “Standup in 8m”, then “Join Standup”.
  • When a call with other people is about to start, the icon pulses. Join is one click away, for Google Meet, Zoom, Teams and Webex links.
  • The panel also lists your later meetings today and opens any of them in Cove.

It’s off by default, and it stays still with Reduce Motion.
