Conversations and files, all in one place

  • Opening an email loads its conversation in the same reading pane. Expand other messages and reply to each one while keeping separate drafts.
  • Preview PDFs, images and text attachments inside the email. Save remains available for files you want to keep or open elsewhere.
  • ⌘Delete now targets the email under your cursor, falling back to the selected email outside the list. The five-second Undo remains.

Attachment previews are limited to supported formats and files up to 25 MiB. Cloud sync remains optional; mobile access and push notifications are not available yet.
