Inbox zero, faster and quieter

  • Work through your inbox from the keyboard: J and K move between emails, E or X archives, S snoozes, H moves to the Inbox, Shift-E marks read, C composes. Press ? to see every shortcut.
  • Choose several emails with Shift-arrows or ⌘A and archive, snooze, flag or trash them together. Z undoes your last change exactly.
  • Snooze offers Later today, This evening, Tomorrow morning, This weekend and Next week. Cove reminds you when an email is back in your inbox (sender and subject only).
  • One click archives everything in Other.
  • Cove opens faster: a large mailbox loads about seven times quicker, and opening an HTML email takes a third of the time.
  • Archiving, marking read or labeling while offline keeps your change and sends it to Gmail when you’re back. Background sync never interrupts you; if Google signs you out, Cove shows one “Sign in to Google again” button.
  • Quitting Cove saves what you were typing and finishes a pending send first.
  • iPhone: snooze with a swipe, a Snoozed folder, offline changes that keep, and a quieter sync.
