Paste images into emails

  • Paste a copied image with ⌘V in a new message or reply. It appears in the attachment list, ready to send.
  • Your message text and selection stay in place. Ordinary text paste works as before.
  • Copied PNG, JPEG and GIF images keep their original format; clipboard TIFF images become PNG.
  • The existing attachment controls and combined 25 MB limit apply.
  • iPhone and iPad also support image Paste in new messages and replies.
