Mail from the keyboard

  • R replies to the open email, E marks it done (archives it and opens the next one), and U marks it read or unread.
  • ⌘Delete still moves it to Trash with Undo, and ↑ ↓ and Esc move around.
  • Shortcuts pause while you type, and the list footer shows them.

Nothing is sent, archived or deleted without you.
