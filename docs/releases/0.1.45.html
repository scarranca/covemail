Smoother scrolling and synced reminders

  • Older mail loads automatically when you reach the end of the list, with an inline retry if loading fails.
  • With the optional cloud pilot enabled, snooze times sync to Cove independently of the recent-mail copy, including reminders for older emails.
  • Offline snooze changes, rescheduling and cancellations are saved and retried safely. The snooze menu shows whether a reminder is local, pending or synced.
  • Tomorrow morning now means the following calendar day at 9 a.m. in your local time zone.

Cloud sync remains off by default. Mobile access and push notifications are not available yet; snoozed mail returns in Cove when due while the app is open, or when you reopen it.
