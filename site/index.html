Faster drafting

Simple rewrites skip planning. With a ChatGPT subscription, drafts stream in as they’re written. Follow-ups like “make it warmer” reuse the emails Cove already found.

  • Ask bigger questions

    Ask Cove can research across Gmail, reading up to 100 matching emails in batches and citing its sources.

  • A daily brief, and more

    Ask for today’s brief, reply from chat, or look up a contact without leaving the conversation.

  • Memories you choose

    Say “remember that…” and Cove uses it in every AI draft. Only what you ask it to keep.

  • Drafts that sound like you

    Cove learns your style from your own sent mail. Only a description of your style is saved, never your emails.

  • New emails and introductions

    Recipients come only from your contacts or addresses you type. Drafts open unsent for review. Cove never sends on its own.

  • A clearer reader

    Conversations as cards, a Reply and Reply all menu, a searchable label picker, and an Unread filter in the Inbox.

  • Calendar in one step

    Connect it with the Google sign-in you already use.

  • Encrypted on your Mac

    Mail is stored encrypted on your Mac, one record per email, so syncing saves only what changed. AI provider keys stay in the macOS Keychain, with optional Touch ID.
