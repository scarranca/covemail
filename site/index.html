A calendar you can drag

Drag on empty space to create an event of that length. Drag an event to move it, even to another day, or drag its bottom edge to resize. Undo is right there, and events with guests ask first.

  • Tasks from your email

    Jev spots promises and requests in mail you send and read. Approve, and they land in Google Tasks with a link back to the email.

  • Ask Cove, inside the email

    A small ✦ opens Ask Cove right where you’re reading. Ask for a reply and the draft lands in the reply box.

  • Tidy many at once

    “Archive these” or “label everything from Maya” shows the exact emails first. Nothing changes until you approve, and Undo is there.

  • Important and Other

    Your Inbox splits what needs you from newsletters and notifications. One click teaches it a sender.

  • Unsubscribe and Spam

    One-click Unsubscribe for newsletters, confirmed first. A Spam folder with Not spam and Report spam, and images stay off.

  • Write with AI, inline

    A ✦ above Send rewrites, shortens or fixes the whole draft or just what you selected. You apply it when it reads right.

  • Set up in one place

    Connections shows what’s ready and what’s next: your AI account, Jev, Calendar and Tasks, each one click away.

  • Encrypted on your Mac

    Mail is stored encrypted on your Mac, one record per email. Keys stay in the macOS Keychain, with optional Touch ID.

Describe it.
Cove builds the steps.

No rules to write. Say what you want the way you’d ask an assistant, check the steps, then try it on your recent mail before it runs.

1Describe the job
“When a supplier sends an invoice, file it under Finance. If it’s overdue, draft a polite reply.”
2Check how it works
Each step reads as When → Then → Says. Open one to change it.
3Try it, then turn it on
See what it would do on the last two weeks of mail. It runs on new emails only, and you can pause it anytime.

Unclear emails wait in Activity for you. Agents never send, delete or pay.
