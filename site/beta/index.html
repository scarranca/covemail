Make yourself at home.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Cove into Applications.
  2. Open Cove from Applications and connect your approved Google account.
  3. Choose your writing provider and configure Jev in Settings when you’re ready.

Jev organization and AI writing use your own provider credentials or supported subscription. Provider access, charges, and usage limits apply.

What’s new in 0.1.44

  • A clearer email reader with better spacing, labeled actions, and distinct Jev and original-email sections.
  • Reply, Forward, and Ask Cove stay within reach while you read, with controls that adapt to narrow panes.
  • Explicit Formatted and Text only modes, follow-up flags and reminders, and translation requests through Ask Cove.

Using Cove 0.1.24 or later? Choose Cove → Check for Updates… to install this release. On 0.1.23 or earlier, install the DMG once to get in-app updates.

Using the beta

Cove is in active development. Features can change, and Google Tasks and GitHub connections are not available yet. An optional private cloud-sync pilot can mirror recent mail from one Mac. A mobile app and sync between devices are not available yet. Keep your original mail in Gmail and review AI suggestions before using them.
